About

I’m a native Bengali speaker and a Master of Education student in Curriculum and Instructional Technology at the Institute of Education and Research, University of Dhaka. My work sits where spoken word, teaching and research meet.

Since October 2024 I have been the Bengali voice-over artist for BRAC’s Stories of Inclusion, recording story-based audio for inclusive education. Alongside voice work, I have taught English, written English learning scripts for rural learners with Monash University researchers, and interviewed participants for university research studies.

I work in Bengali and English.
